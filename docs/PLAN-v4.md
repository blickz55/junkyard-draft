# PLAN-v4 — v0.7 "Play with people", re-cut after 0.6.0

Written 2026-10-30 at `main` after the 0.6.0 release (verifier protocol 2, rules version 3). It replaces the v0.7 section of `docs/PLAN-v1.md`; v0.8 onward stays as PLAN-v1 describes until PLAN-v5. Same format as PLAN-v2 and PLAN-v3: binding policies first, then numbered, sized increments with *What / Why / Accept*. Sizes: S ≤ half a day, M a day or two, L longer. ⚑ needs a human. **No rules change in v0.7**: `RULES_VERSION` stays 3, every increment is replay-compatible, the verifier's protocol grows additively to 3.

## 0. Where we start

Shipped in 0.6.0: identity without passwords (`Name#TAG`, recovery code, one best entry per player per seed, `DELETE /me`), the minted daily with a published commitment, the 48-hour window with freeze and archive, streak with grace, medals at freeze, the generated fairness policy, and the replay reveal gate (PLAN-v1's 0.7.6, pulled into v0.6 as integrity). Measured in 0.6.7: the JSON board crosses the 5 MB trigger at about 1,000 daily players within a month, or 100 players within a year, because every daily is a new seed (~85 KB per day per 100 players; a stored replay ~700 B, an entry ~310 B).

Still open, and still the gate: **the verifier is not hosted (0.6.8 ⚑)**. Everything below is built and tested against the local verifier and CI's Docker image, exactly as v0.6 was, and none of it is true for players until one server mints the daily for everyone. v0.7 adds the features that make a hosted verifier worth visiting with a friend; it does not add anything that needs a crowd.

What this re-cut changes against PLAN-v1's v0.7: the storage decision comes first because groups and challenges add rows; the **weekly seed ships without modifiers** (a modifier is a rule parameter, so it is a `RULES_VERSION` bump on a `rules-v4` branch; it is a candidate for the post-launch cadence, PLAN-v1 1.0.4, once there are players to plan a week around); a share card replaces PLAN-v1's implicit "share line" because the verified standing is now a thing worth a picture; the first cut of name moderation (PLAN-v1 0.9.3) moves up because groups add a second user-written name field and the 0.6.0 blocklist is a stub; the reveal gate is already done.

## 1. Policies that bind every increment

**Hosting is the gate for ranked play** (PLAN-v3 §1, unchanged). The title keeps saying "local verifier" until 0.6.8 ⚑ is done. Nothing in v0.7 may remove the local path: a player with no server still has the practice daily, local bests, the archive of their own runs, and a fork or a share card of their own run.

**Everything social is keyed by `player_id` and nothing by name.** Groups, challenges, reports and reactions reference the SHA-256 player id; names are labels resolved at read time, so a rename follows the player everywhere and a deletion anonymises everywhere. Anonymous entries (no identity) can be watched and challenged but never grouped, reported or credited.

**Every user-written string goes through the one name filter.** Group names and any label a player types pass `nameProblem()` (normalisation, length, folded blocklist) at the verifier before anything is stored, and the moderation flow of 0.7.7 covers every such field in one test that pushes a blocked string through each (the bugnet lesson: fields added later and never wired in). No free text anywhere else: challenge messages and reactions are pre-set phrases or nothing.

**Reveal-gate consistency.** On an open daily a challenge link carries the challenger's per-round earnings (derived server-side) and never their replay or layout; the full replay stays behind the 0.6.6 gate until the recipient has submitted or the day closed. Forks are made only from replays the player may already watch.

**Storage.** The decision rule from 0.6.7 stands: above 5 MB on disk or 50 ms per commit on the hosting VPS, the next storage increment moves. 0.7.0 does the small change first (a hot/cold split plus replay compaction) and writes the projected numbers; 0.7.0b (SQLite behind the same `Board` interface with a one-shot importer) is scheduled, not optional, the moment a measured beta month shows the hot file over the trigger. Single writer only; the runbook says so.

**Abuse baseline for the new surfaces.** Group codes are 8 base32 characters (40 bits) and never enumerable; at most 5 groups per player and 50 members per group; every new POST has a per-player bucket (groups 5/min, reports 5/min, reactions 10/min) beside the per-IP limiter; a report hides the target's name pending review and is never a ban; duplicate-hash rejection as today. Leagues, divisions and anything needing a population of 300 daily submitters stay out (PLAN-v1 "Do not do").

**Protocol compatibility.** Additive; `/health` carries `protocol: 3`. A 0.6.0 client keeps working for one release: no field it reads changes meaning, no endpoint it calls changes shape.

**Evidence.** Every increment: unit tests on the verifier, an e2e against a spawned verifier where the client changed, the Docker smoke extended when an endpoint is added, STATUS numbers, instrumented copies of a flaky test rather than guesses, and no fabricated populations anywhere.

## 2. The increments, in dependency order

**0.7.0 Storage: hot/cold split and replay compaction** — M. *What:* the live `board.json` keeps open seeds and the last 30 days of frozen dailies ("hot"); older frozen days move at startup and once a day to monthly cold files (`cold/YYYY-MM.json`: entries, the day record, medals, par, and only the top 10 stored replays — lower replays are dropped and their entries say `replayAvailable: false`); `/archive`, `/board`, `/histogram` and `/replay` read cold files lazily through a small LRU so final boards stay fully served; the commit path writes the hot file only; `tools/storage-measure.ts` gains `--split` and reports hot-file size and commit time for 100/1,000 players × 365 days; `server/README.md` records the projection and the SQLite trigger status. Nothing a player can see changes except that replays below the top 10 on a month-old day are gone (the policy page says so). *Why:* the 0.6.7 numbers: entries, not replays, dominate at 1,000 players (30,000 × 310 B in a month) and days, not players, drive growth; bounding the rewritten file to a 30-day window makes commit latency flat while keeping every final board; SQLite remains the answer at scale and is the next line. *Accept:* tests — split is idempotent and never touches an open or recently frozen day; a cold day's board, histogram, medals and top-10 replays are served exactly as before the move; a replay below the top 10 answers 404 with a reason; a restart finds the cold files; the measure tool shows 100 players × 365 days with a hot file under 5 MB and commit under 50 ms on the dev PC, and names the 1,000-player number honestly; the rollback rehearsal still passes.

**0.7.0b SQLite behind the `Board` interface** — L, conditional. *What:* `server/store-sqlite.ts` implementing load/commit/queries with WAL and a one-shot importer from the JSON files; chosen by `STORE=sqlite`; the JSON path stays for tests and small deployments. *Why:* PLAN-v1 0.8.7's reasoning; triggered by measurement, not taste. *Accept:* the verifier test suite runs against both stores; importer round-trips a 0.6.7 synthetic board; `/health` reports the store.

**0.7.1 Challenge link: "beat this"** — M. *What:* any verified entry (the player's own, or one they may watch) exports a web URL `…/?challenge=<replayHash>` (and the desktop `jd://` form); the verifier gains `GET /entry?hash=` returning `{ seed, score, player, rounds[], frozen, medals }` where `rounds[]` are the per-round earnings derived at verification (stored on the entry like `finalMachines`, backfilled from stored replays at startup); the recipient plays the same seed with the challenger's earnings overlaid live per round ("Challenger: $120 this round · you: $98") and sees a two-row comparison on the results; a challenge on a daily inside its window counts toward the daily board through the normal verified path; the detailed share line says "beat <name>'s $X" or "short of <name>'s $X by $Y". No new storage beyond the `rounds` field. *Why:* PLAN-v1 0.7.1 (Balatro "beat my seed" threads, Trackmania ghosts); it needs exactly two people and no server-side state, so it is the first social feature that works the day the verifier is hosted. *Accept:* verifier — `rounds` derived and backfilled, `/entry` for an unknown hash is 404, for an open daily it carries earnings and no actions; e2e — open a challenge link, overlay from round 1, finish, two-row result, the recipient's run verifies independently and lands on the daily board when in window; the overlay never appears on an unrelated seed.

**0.7.2 Groups: a code that scopes the board** — M. *What:* `POST /groups { name }` (bearer; the name passes the filter; returns `{ code, name }`), `POST /groups/join { code }`, `POST /groups/leave { code }`, `GET /groups` (bearer: my groups with member counts and names); `Board.groups` keyed by code with `playerIds[]`, caps 5 per player and 50 per group; `/board`, `/histogram`, `/daily` and `/archive` accept `group=<code>` from a bearer who is a member and filter entries to members' player ids (ranks and percentiles recomputed over the filtered set, never over the global one); the title strip and results board gain a "Board: Everyone / <group>" selector remembered per device; a group's creator can rename (filtered, cooldown) and disband (members keep their entries). No chat, no invitations beyond sharing the code. *Why:* PLAN-v1 0.7.2 (friends-first boards are the trustworthy default; Steam's "Friends" board scope emulated for the web); a code is the smallest possible social graph. *Accept:* verifier — create/join/leave/caps, filtered histogram equals a hand-computed one, a non-member asking for a group board is 403, a blocked group name is 422, deletion of a player removes them from groups; e2e — two browser contexts, one creates and one joins, both see a two-entry group board and a different percentile than the global one.

**0.7.3 Fork from round N into practice** — S/M. *What:* from any watched replay (verified or own) the results and the replay HUD offer "Continue from round N"; `forkFrom(replay, n)` in `game/src/sim/` returns the run state after round N as a fresh practice run on the same seed with `forkedFrom: { hash, player, round }`; forks are labelled in the HUD ("forked from Jon#7Q2M after round 3 · practice") and in the results, can never be submitted (the submit button is absent, the dev API refuses), and record local bests under a separate "forks" bucket so they never masquerade as the player's own run. *Why:* PLAN-v1 0.7.3 (others' solutions as material, Opus Magnum and Slay the Spire habits) while keeping copied solutions off the boards. *Accept:* sim test — fork state equals the replay's state after round N for every golden replay and every N; the fork's remaining rounds re-simulate deterministically; `exportReplay` of a fork is refused; e2e — fork from the watched replay, play one round, see the label, no submit control.

**0.7.4 Share card** — S. *What:* on the results, "Share card" renders a PNG (canvas, 1200×630) with the seed, score, six round glyphs, the verified standing when `verifiedHash` is set ("#3 of 41 · verified by re-simulation"), the medal when the day is frozen, the group rank when a group board is selected, and the challenge line when a challenge was played; local runs render "local run · not verified"; downloaded like the GIF, never uploaded; a URL only when the share-link option is on. *Why:* a picture of a verified standing is what gets posted; the text share stays for chat. *Accept:* e2e — the PNG has the expected size and the footer text is read back from the canvas (as the GIF footer is today); a local run's card never contains "verified".

**0.7.5 Weekly seed (no modifier)** — M. *What:* the verifier mints `weekly-YYYY-Www-xxxxxxxx` from the same secret, opens it Friday 18:00 UTC with a commitment published on the daily of that day, closes it after 7 days, freezes it, sets medals and archives it through the same pipeline as dailies (`dailies` generalises to `events` with `kind: 'daily' | 'weekly'`; the JSON key stays for compatibility); `/weekly` mirrors `/daily`; the title shows a second strip line ("This week's seed · closes in 5 d · provisional"); the streak is daily-only; the share card and groups work on it unchanged. No modifiers: the rules do not change. *Why:* a longer window gives people who miss a day something to plan around over the weekend (PLAN-v1 0.7.4's cadence argument) without the rules bump a modifier needs; modifiers are a `rules-v4` candidate for the post-launch cadence once there are players. *Accept:* verifier — mint, open/close boundaries at Friday 18:00 UTC with a fake clock, commitment chain, freeze and medals, archive lists weeklies with their kind; e2e — the weekly strip from the spawned verifier, a submission lands on the weekly board and not the daily one; `tools/mint-check.ts` checks the weekly chain too.

**0.7.6 Discord webhook from the verifier** — S. *What:* `DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL` (optional): on a new daily or weekly seed, a new #1 on an open event, and a freeze (with medal thresholds), the verifier POSTs one short message; one message per event type per seed; honours 429 and `Retry-After`; failures are logged and never affect a submission; a dry-run flag prints instead of posting. *Why:* PLAN-v1 0.7.5 (a daily thread makes the ritual visible without hosting a bot). *Accept:* verifier test with a mocked webhook — the three triggers fire once each, a 429 is retried after the header's delay, a 500 is dropped and logged, and `/verify` latency is unaffected (the post is fire-and-forget).

**0.7.7 Name moderation, first cut** — S/M. *What:* `POST /report { playerId | hash, reason }` (bearer; reasons from a fixed list; 5/min) hides the reported name as "player" on every board until a moderator acts and never touches scores; `tools/moderate.ts` lists reports, clears or forces a rename (a cleared name may be re-reported once more at most), and appends to `moderation.jsonl`; every user-written field (display name, group name) is enumerated in one test that pushes a blocked string through each; the policy page gains the report path and the appeal address placeholder (the owner fills it in). *Why:* groups add a second name field and the 0.6.0 blocklist is a stub; PLAN-v1 0.9.3's reasoning, pulled forward only as far as the new fields need. *Accept:* verifier — report hides, clear restores, a forced rename follows the player onto every board, the audit row exists; the enumeration test; e2e — a hidden name reads "player" on the title strip.

**0.7.8 ⚑ Host the verifier and open the web beta** — human (unchanged from PLAN-v3 0.6.8): domain, one small VPS with Caddy and the Docker image (`DATA_DIR` on the volume, `DAILY_SECRET` backed up, `CORS_ORIGIN` the site, optionally `DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL`), `VERIFIER_URL` baked into the web and desktop builds, Cloudflare Pages, the e2e daily submission against the hosted URL recorded in STATUS, and after one month `tools/storage-measure.ts`'s projection checked against the real hot file (the 0.7.0b trigger).

## 3. What the nightly routine may take

0.7.0–0.7.7 are `main` work with no rules change; each is one commit that changes client and server together where the protocol grows, extends the Docker smoke when it adds an endpoint, and regenerates the policy page when a constant or a rule of conduct changes. 0.7.0b runs only when a measured month says so. 0.7.8 is the owner's. PLAN-v1's v0.8 (engines, scaling, gamepad, screen reader, remapping, strings, Steam plumbing) waits for PLAN-v5 and for the beta's first numbers.

## 4. Metrics and risks

Watch (all from consented funnel events and verifier counts, never from names): share of verified daily submitters who open a challenge link in a week (target ≥ 20 %); share of players in at least one group after a month of beta (target ≥ 30 %); share cards exported per verified run; weekly-seed submitters as a share of daily ones (target ≥ 40 %); report volume and time to clear (target < 72 h, PLAN-v1's beta gate). Risks: building social features before anyone can use them means the first real test is the beta itself (mitigation: every increment has a two-context e2e, and nothing here needs a crowd); the hot/cold split adds a second place data lives (mitigation: one `Board` interface, the rehearsal script extended to a cold month); group boards recompute percentiles over small sets (mitigation: the histogram already says "N verified runs" and shows nothing below five); the webhook can leak a seed early if misconfigured (mitigation: it posts only what `/daily` already serves).
