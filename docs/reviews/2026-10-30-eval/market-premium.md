# What made the premium games in Junkyard Draft's family sell — market research for the product interview and the 1.0 roadmap

Compiled 2026-10-29. Sources are linked after each fact. **est.** = third-party model (Gamalytic, Raijin, steamdata.ai, Boxleiter calculators), never developer-disclosed; **inf.** = our inference. Steam store pages show English-only review counts; Raijin/ITAD show all languages, so counts differ by source. The shared web-search budget for this session ran out before a last verification pass; items we could not confirm are marked **not verified** rather than guessed, and the appendix lists them.

Junkyard Draft as it stands (from `docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/STATUS.md`): six rounds, one draft of three machines per round, 8×6 grid, 80-tick deterministic run, ~5–8 minutes per run; 7 pool machines (hopper, conveyor, splitter, crusher, furnace, plate press, dock) plus the v0.5 Tuner and a shelved experimental Sieve; 5 materials; a reroll; a replay-verified daily and weekly seed with a histogram and bot par, medals, forgiving streak, groups by code, challenge links, forks, replay watching, clips and share cards, name moderation; reduced motion and three text sizes; no accounts; 0.7.0 released, v0.8 (PLAN-v5) covers cross-engine determinism, handheld preset, remapping, gamepad, screen-reader mirror, localisation, verifier hardening, Linux build, custom Phaser build and Steam plumbing.

### Balatro (LocalThunk / Playstack, Feb 2024)
- $14.99, shallow discounts (20% typical) — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/Balatro/), [steamdata.ai](https://steamdata.ai/game/2379780/balatro). PC + all consoles day one; mobile and Apple Arcade Sep 2024; Switch 2 Feb 2026 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatro).
- Funnel: public demo Jun 2023 (2,440 wishlists end of June) → Next Fest Oct 2023 (77K) → Next Fest Feb 2024 → 208K wishlists on launch day, 119K Steam units day one; streamers Northernlion and Dan Gheesling — [LocalThunk timeline](https://localthunk.com/blog/balatro-timeline-3aarh). No early access.
- 500K in 10 days, 1M in a month, **5M by Jan 2025** (official); mobile ~$4.4M gross in two months — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/balatro-sells-5-million-copies-after-end-of-year-spike), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatro). Steam est. 4.6–5.3M units — [steamdata.ai](https://steamdata.ai/game/2379780/balatro). 98% of 105K English reviews, ~167K all languages — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/balatro/steam-reviews).
- Run 30–60 min — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4634861275507086821). Content at 1.0: 150 Jokers, 15 decks, 8 stakes, 22 Tarot, 11 Planet, 32 Vouchers, 20 challenges — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/Balatro/). Post-launch: one balance patch, four free cosmetic "Friends of Jimbo" packs; 1.1 delayed indefinitely since Sep 2025 — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/localthunk-delays-balatro-update-to-avoid-crunch-and-burnout).
- **No daily mode, no leaderboards.** Seeded runs and challenges exist but disable unlocks and stats; players complain that this is not flagged — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/?p=970716), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4338734550484225211). Meta: Jokers/decks/stakes unlock through play.
- Solo developer, pixel art himself, music commissioned via Fiverr, ~26 months — [LocalThunk timeline](https://localthunk.com/blog/balatro-timeline-3aarh). Accessibility: reduced motion, high-contrast suits, no remap/text size documented; Deck players asked for larger text — [Family Gaming Database](https://www.familygamingdatabase.com/accessibility/Balatro), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4346607305584559403).
- Praised: addictive loop, quality, the poker × roguelike blend. Criticised: RNG dominance, unfair losses, few viable high-stake strategies — [Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/2379780/balatro). Pitfalls: PEGI 18 re-rating for gambling imagery, overturned to 12 in 2025; burnout — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatro), [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/localthunk-recalls-the-huge-pressure-mental-health-issues-while-working-on-balatro/).

### Opus Magnum and the Zachtronics catalogue
- $19.99, rarely discounted; EA Oct 2017 → 1.0 Dec 2017; GOG refused it at first for "resemblance to a mobile game"; Switch Mar 2026 with a $12.99 DLC — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opus_Magnum_(video_game)), [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/3107410/). 97% of 3.7K; Metacritic 90. ~36 campaign puzzles in 5 chapters, ~20 h — [Punished Backlog](https://punishedbacklog.com/opus-magnum-the-gold-standard-of-puzzle-games/).
- **Histogram leaderboards**: after each level a histogram per metric (cost/cycles/area) with your bar and your Steam friends listed; no global named list, because, per the SpaceChem postmortem, "Getting your name at the top of the leaderboards is a fantastic incentive for cheating" — [Linux Game Consortium postmortem](https://linuxgameconsortium.com/postmortem-zachtronics-industries-spacechem/). The community then built its own global record trackers that require a GIF per submission — [leaderboard bot](https://github.com/F43nd1r/zachtronics-leaderboard-bot), [Biggie blog](https://biggieblog.com/tracking-the-global-opus-magnum-records/).
- **GIF export** was the marketing: "the gifs are actually the closest thing to our elevator pitch" — [PCGamesInsider](https://www.pcgamesinsider.biz/success-story/66636/). Team ~5, ~1 year per game. Barth: puzzle games are "not an evergreen game… people pick them up, play them and finish them and then move on"; studio closed Jul 2022 — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/zachtronics-farewell-goodbye-closing-eliza-last-call-bb-1849096767). Catalogue: SpaceChem only 2% finished its 50 puzzles and its 4-hour demo was "not good for encouraging impulse buys"; TIS-100 at $7 "too cheap to be super-successful" yet outsold Infinifactory 2:1 — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/zachtronics-i-shenzhen-i-o-i-is-a-game-for-people-who-code-games).

Rewind ($19.99, free to owners) shipped a **free "Daily Demo" app** that refreshes with a new dungeon and class each day with its own leaderboard — [Steam Daily Demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2232060/Desktop_Dungeons_Rewind__Daily_Demo/). Rewind 75% of 503 vs the original's 88%: the 3D remake split the audience — [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/14790/desktop-dungeons-rewind). "Long tail sales are pretty flat" — [QCF blog](https://www.qcfdesign.com/).

### Tetris Effect: Connected (Enhance, 2018 → Steam 2021)
- $39.99, routinely −50%; per-mode global/friends Steam leaderboards; the **Weekend Ritual** (Sat 12:00–Sun 12:00 UTC) pools everyone's points toward a community goal that unlocks a hidden stage, still running weekly in 2025 — [tetris.wiki](https://tetris.wiki/Tetris_Effect). 94% of 8.5K; Metacritic 88–94. Accessibility: Zone Brilliance intensity, Guideline colours, full remapping.

### Dicey Dungeons (Terry Cavanagh, Aug 2019)
- itch paid alpha ($7.99) with fortnightly builds → $14.99 Steam/itch; first month outsold his previous two Steam games combined — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2019/11/dicey-dungeons-outsold-terry-cavanaghs-last-two-steam-games-in-the-first-month/). Est. 0.3–0.8M; 89% of ~12K. 6 characters × 6 episodes; Halloween Special and the free Reunion DLC (2022, 6 episodes) built with the mod system — [Nintendo Life](https://nintendolife.com/news/2022/06/terry-cavanaghs-dicey-dungeons-rolls-out-huge-new-free-dlc-reunion-soon). No daily or leaderboards; no colourblind mode (community mod). Team of 5, 18 months. Music Chipzel.

## 5. Ten product ideas transplantable without breaking the constraints

Each keeps "same seed for everyone, deterministic, replay-verified, no dark patterns": nothing below changes offers or scoring on a shared seed, and everything reduces to actions the verifier already re-simulates or to labels on top of verified entries.

1. **Looping factory GIF of the round-6 layout, with the score and seed baked in.** Precedent: Opus Magnum's GIF export was the studio's entire marketing ("closest thing to our elevator pitch"); Mini Metro added GIF export in its eighth year — [PCGamesInsider](https://www.pcgamesinsider.biz/success-story/66636/), [Miniversary](https://dinopoloclub.com/2023/07/24/the-miniversary-update-is-out-now-available-for-mini-metro-mini-motorways). JD's sim is deterministic, so the GIF is rendered from the replay offline; it carries the verified hash as a caption, which the Zachtronics community had to bolt on by hand.

2. **Friends-first histogram and a "beat your friend" nudge in the daily.** Precedent: Zachtronics' histograms with friends listed, Mini Metro and Motorways converting their leaderboards to histograms; Islanders' most common leaderboard complaint is the missing friends view — [Linux Game Consortium](https://linuxgameconsortium.com/postmortem-zachtronics-industries-spacechem/), [Islanders reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1046030/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated). JD's groups already do this; at 1.0 populate the group from Steam friends automatically and show the friend's bar on the same histogram.

3. **A maps/grids catalogue unlocked by milestones, never affecting the daily grid.** Precedent: Mini Metro's 34 cities and Motorways' 25, each unlocked by thresholds and each a free update that re-launches the game; Balatro's 15 decks × 8 stakes. These are the "big number" on the store page. For JD: grids with pre-placed walls, docks on different edges, 7×7 or 10×5, each a seed namespace (rules change, so `RULES_VERSION` bump with the golden corpus) — [Wikipedia Mini Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mini_Metro_(video_game)).

4. **Stakes / ascension ladder for the free-play seed, with the daily fixed at one stake.** Precedent: StS's 20 Ascensions and Balatro's 8 stakes are the replay engines; Against the Storm's 24 difficulty levels earned "flexible difficulty" praise — [Steam Balatro](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/Balatro/). Stakes are deterministic modifiers (fewer ticks, dearer rerolls, a tax per machine) encoded in the seed string, so a stake-5 replay verifies like any other.

5. **A weekly "modifier daily" (one day a week uses a modifier set) and a monthly long-form challenge.** Precedent: Mini Motorways made one daily per week an Expert-mode day; Dorfromantik's Monthly mode; Tetris Effect's Weekend Ritual with a *community* goal — [DPC blog](https://dinopoloclub.com/?p=2247), [tetris.wiki](https://tetris.wiki/Tetris_Effect). JD's verifier already mints seeds with commitments, so a modifier is just more seed namespace. A community total ("everyone shipped $N this weekend → unlock a palette") is a no-dark-pattern collective reward.

6. **A permanent free daily build ("Daily Demo") on web and Steam, separate from the paid app.** Precedent: Desktop Dungeons Rewind's Daily Demo app that refreshes each day with its own leaderboard; Factorio's permanent demo; Loop Hero's demo still live; Valve's position that demos do not hurt visibility — [Steam Daily Demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2232060/Desktop_Dungeons_Rewind__Daily_Demo/), [HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/03/07/why-demos-dont-hurt-your-visibility/). The web version is already this; the Steam demo listing is the missing wishlist collector.

7. **Run history and a "collection" screen of every machine, material and medal, with per-seed stats.** Precedent: Balatro's collection and stats; StS's run history that spawned community trackers; Backpack Battles' build history — [STS Tracker](https://ststracker.app/about). Pure client-side labelling over local replays; export as JSON for community sites (no accounts needed).

8. **Contracts: hand-authored fixed-draft puzzles with a par and a histogram.** Precedent: Balatro's 20 challenges, Automachef's Contracts mode, Mini Motorways' per-city challenges in the Challenge City update that produced the largest post-launch bump — [Games Press](https://gamespress.com/Best-Selling-Strategy-Sim-Mini-Motorways-Speeds-into-the-Holiday-Seaso). JD's `tutorial-*` namespace already constrains drafts; a `contract-*` namespace with fixed offers is the same mechanism and gives a countable number ("40 contracts") for the store page.

9. **Blueprint / layout strings.** Precedent: Factorio blueprint strings, shapez 2 blueprint library, Opus Magnum's Workshop requirement that puzzles be proven solvable — [FFF-449](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-449). In JD a layout string is a partial action list; pasting it into a seed replays the placements that are legal there and stops at the first that is not, so it never bypasses rule checks and is still submitted as a normal replay.

10. **A named post-launch cadence: one free "drop" every 6–8 weeks, each a new grid, contract set or machine behind a `RULES_VERSION` bump, announced on a public roadmap.** Precedent: Motorways' free city every 2–4 months for five years; Against the Storm's two-week EA cadence earning "EA done right"; the opposite — Loop Hero, Backpack Hero, Cobalt Core — where "no updates" became the top review theme within 18 months — [Vaporlens Loop Hero](https://vaporlens.app/app/1282730/loop_hero), [Vaporlens Backpack Hero](https://vaporlens.app/app/1970580/backpack_hero.md). JD's version-pinned verifier (old replays stay verified under their rules version) makes this safer than for any comparable.

Honourable mentions, not costed: a screen-reader-complete daily (LbaL is the only comparable with one, and it is praised); a physical component (Dorfromantik's board game sold 500K and won Spiel des Jahres); Steam trading cards and a soundtrack SKU (Loop Hero).

## 6. Pitfalls the comparables hit

- **Short total content and the two-hour refund.** Steam refunds anything under 2 h / 14 days — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds/). Paddle Paddle Paddle (Jul 2026, $5, <2 h) saw a 21% refund rate at 90% positive — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/dev-tells-valve-to-fix-steams-exploitable-2-hour-refund-policy-as-over-55-000-players-refund-his-short-game-and-even-brag-about-it-in-reviews/). Baseline ~10% (GDCo survey). JD's 5–8-minute runs are not the risk; "I saw everything in 90 minutes" is. Dailies, stakes, grids and contracts are the fix, not longer runs. Mini Metro reported a low return rate with the same run length — [postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/postmortem-dinosaur-polo-club-s-i-mini-metro-i-).
- **Puzzle-genre weakness on Steam.** Puzzle is 2.8% of strategy-genre revenue among $1M+ games; Puzzle→Logic demos reach 10+ CCU 3% of the time at Next Fest vs 19.5% for Action RPG; "lowest median earnings" cluster includes puzzle — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-steam-revenue-distribution), [HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/10/21/how-sloppy-is-the-october-2026-steam-next-fest/). Freshly Frosted (98% positive, Metacritic 85, est. ~$67K) and Automachef (75%, est. ~$158K) are the genre's cautionary cases; Zach Barth closed Zachtronics partly because puzzle games are not evergreen — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/zachtronics-farewell-goodbye-closing-eliza-last-call-bb-1849096767). Tag and trailer JD as Automation × Roguelike Deckbuilder × Score Attack; "puzzle" only as a secondary tag.
- **Next Fest timing.** Wishlists gained correlate r=0.825 with pre-fest wishlists; median gains 322 (<1K pre-fest) vs 5,215 (10–100K); demos live well before the fest earned ~2.5× — [HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/03/26/benchmarks-how-many-wishlists-can-i-get-from-steam-next-fest/). Balatro did two Next Fests and launched within weeks of the second; Cobalt Core launched a month after its fest; shapez 2's demo was live two weeks before its fest and stayed up after — all consistent. Do not enter the first fest with a bare page.
- **Over-generous or over-long demos.** SpaceChem's 4-hour demo was "not good for encouraging impulse buys" — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/zachtronics-i-shenzhen-i-o-i-is-a-game-for-people-who-code-games). Counter-evidence: Backpack Battles' 21-month demo and shapez's full free web game both fed Steam. Reconciliation (inf.): a demo that is *the daily* (fresh every day, no catalogue) converts; a demo that is the first third of a finite campaign satisfies. JD's free web daily is the former.
- **Price too low.** TIS-100 at $7 "too cheap to be super-successful"; Islanders at $4.99 sold 1M but its $9.99 sequel found a tenth of the audience; shapez launched at $3.99 and had to climb; Stacklands, Dorfromantik, Against the Storm, StS all raised prices without review damage. Zukowski: price at comparables +20%, discount ≤20% for six months, 50–75% only at major events, "if you discount too deep, you just gave away your game to everybody who would have bought it at 25% off" — [PreMortem](https://premortem.games/2024/07/22/chris-zukowskis-tips-on-how-to-not-screw-up-your-game-launch-wishlists-arent-milk/). Counter-pressure: GDCo reports top-50 median launch price fell to ~$15.64 by units (Feb 2023 → Oct 2025) and "charging above $25 is getting trickier" — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-people-are-spending).
- **Price too high for visible content.** Cobalt Core at $19.99 (~1 h runs, "limited content") and Tetris Effect at $39.99 (price is three of the top five negative themes) show the ceiling; community "fair price" sits $3–7 under MSRP for Backpack Hero/Battles — [Vaporlens Cobalt Core](https://vaporlens.app/app/2179850/cobalt_core), [Vaporlens Tetris Effect](https://vaporlens.app/app/1003590/tetris_effect_connected.md).
- **Silent seeded-run penalties.** Balatro players dislike that seeded/challenge runs silently disable unlocks — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4338734550484225211). JD's rule that progression never touches scoring should be stated on screen, not just in the policy page.
- **Platform ports that arrive years late or break.** Loop Hero: Switch +9 months, Xbox +2 years, mobile +3 years; Backpack Battles and Tetris Effect carry "Deck broken" review themes; Mini Motorways had a Switch lock-up tied to the daily — [Vaporlens Backpack Battles](https://vaporlens.app/app/2427700/backpack_battles.md). Deck Verified matters less for sales than for avoiding the negative theme: Deck is ~0.5–0.8% of Steam users and Valve says Verified mainly buys placement — [Steam HW Survey](https://store.steampowered.com/hwsurvey/), [GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/steam-deck-what-you-should-know-about).
- **Gambling-imagery ratings.** Balatro (PEGI 18, reversed) and Luck be a Landlord (Google Play ban in 13 countries). JD's "draft" and "reroll" are not casino imagery, but avoid slot/card framing in art and copy — [Wikipedia LbaL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luck_Be_a_Landlord).
- **Review bombs from balance churn and from one-line credits.** StS2's three waves; Backpack Battles' recent-review slide as the ladder balance churns — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/slay-the-spire-2-is-fantastic-so-why-is-it-being-review-bombed/1100-6539724/). JD's version-pinned replays let old dailies stay valid under old rules, which removes the "my run is now invalid" grievance.
- **Sequel-to-a-cheap-game ceiling and remakes that split the audience.** Islanders: New Shores and Desktop Dungeons Rewind — the audience anchored on the first price and look.
- **Early-access that outruns its plan.** Backpack Battles planned 3–6 months, took 15; Backpack Hero slipped 6 months and shipped a story mode nobody wanted; Mini Metro promised "2–3 months" for a year — [Wikipedia Backpack Battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack_Battles), [postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/postmortem-dinosaur-polo-club-s-i-mini-metro-i-). State an EA scope in features, not dates.

## 7. Price band and platform sequencing the evidence supports (2026–2027)

**Price.** The comparables with Junkyard Draft's run length and team size cluster at **$9.99–$14.99**: Mini Metro/Motorways $9.99, Luck be a Landlord $9.99, Dorfromantik $13.99, Balatro/Loop Hero/Dicey $14.99, Backpack Battles $14.99. Above that, $19.99 needs visible volume (Cobalt Core's reviews punish it; Backpack Hero's "fair price" is $10–12) and $24.99+ is for 30-hour sandboxes or sequels with an installed base (shapez 2, StS, Against the Storm). Market-wide, the median launch price of top-50 new releases fell to ~$15.64 by units and discount events are ~4× less effective at generating impressions than in 2019 — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-people-are-spending), [GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-shape-of-the-pc-new-game-market). Every comparable that raised its price (six cases) kept its score; the two cases of under-pricing (TIS-100, Islanders) are regretted by their developers or constrained the sequel.

Recommendation (inf. from the above): **$9.99 for a Steam early-access or "1.0-lite" launch with a stated rise to $14.99 at 1.0** once the grid catalogue, stakes and contracts exist; launch discount 10–20% for two weeks (Backpack Battles, Dorfromantik, Tetris Effect all did this); no discount deeper than 20% for the first six months; 33–50% only in Summer/Winter sales thereafter. The free web daily stays free forever (Factorio-demo logic). Paid DLC only after 12+ months and only as content (a grid pack or contract set at $3.99–$4.99 like Dorfromantik and Stacklands), never as power.

**Platform sequencing.**
1. **Web (free daily, now)** — collecting wishlists requires a Steam page, so create the page and a Steam demo listing as soon as the game is presentable; the web build is the marketing engine (shapez, Backpack Battles, Mini Metro) and Zukowski's itch→Steam cases show no cannibalisation — [HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/22/more-games-that-made-the-itch-io-to-steam-transition/).
2. **Steam Next Fest** only with 5K+ wishlists already on the page (Bronze threshold) and the demo live two weeks before — [HTMAG benchmarks](https://howtomarketagame.com/benchmarks/).
3. **Steam Windows + Linux at launch (Deck Verified via the Linux build in PLAN-v5)**, macOS when the determinism corpus is green on WebKit — Deck Verified is cheap for an Electron/web game and prevents a negative review theme; it is not a sales driver on its own — [GDCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/steam-deck-what-you-should-know-about).
4. **Switch / Switch 2 5–12 months after Steam** via a porting publisher (Dorfromantik +5 months, Mini Metro +33 months, Loop Hero +9, Backpack Hero day-and-date) — [Wikipedia Dorfromantik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorfromantik). Indies still make 60–65% on Steam even with a day-one Switch version — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-which-games-are-successful).
5. **Mobile ~12 months after 1.0, premium not F2P, once demand is proven** (Balatro's mobile ≈ $4.4M in two months but only after 3.5M PC/console sales; LbaL and shapez via Playdigious). Mobile is where the daily format is strongest (Motorways' 1.48M daily plays were on Apple Arcade) and where gambling-imagery ratings bite, so keep the industrial framing — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/balatro-exceeds-5-million-in-sales-on-all-platforms/).
6. **Localisation before the Steam launch, CJK first**: China was 48% of Backpack Battles' and 32% of Stacklands' sales; Japan 11% of Backpack Battles — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/04/25/backpack-battles-sales-640k-copies-china-top-country).
