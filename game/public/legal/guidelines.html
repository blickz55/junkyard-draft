Community guidelines (v0)

Junkyard Draft · last changed 2026-10-30 · rules version 4, verifier protocol 2

The short version: be someone the other players want on the same board. These guidelines govern names, play on the ranked boards, sharing, groups and reports; they grow with the social features.

Names

Pick a handle, not a real name, yours or anyone else's. No slurs, no sexual content, no harassment aimed at a person or a group, no impersonation of other players, of the game's staff or of a brand. Look-alike characters are folded before the blocklist is checked, so a disguised word is still the word. Group names follow the same rule. 32 characters at most.

Play

Ranked boards are for runs you played yourself, in the game. Do not automate the daily or the weekly, do not feed replays from a solver, do not hold several places with several keys. Practice seeds, forks and challenges are the place to experiment. The par line is a bot: a target, not a person to outrank.

Found an exploit?

A trick the simulation allows is a rule: use it, and tell us if it looks unintended. A way to make the verifier accept something the simulation would not is a bug: do not use it on a ranked board; report it to contact@junkyarddraft.com and get credit in the changelog.

Sharing

Share cards, challenge links and clips exist to be shared. On an open daily share your result, not your layout: the reveal rules hide replays until the day closes so that nobody copies a line, and posting a layout defeats them.

Groups

A group code is an invitation: anyone who has it can join and see the group's boards, so post it where you want everyone reading to join. The creator may rename or disband a group; leaving is always possible.

Reporting

Report a name from any board or replay with one of the fixed reasons. Reports are counted, not read as text: 2 established players agreeing hide a name until a moderator looks. Do not report scores you dislike, and do not organise mass reports: that is itself a breach.

Consequences and appeals

In order of weight: a hidden name, a forced rename, removal of entries, a block. Appeals go to moderation@junkyarddraft.com with your tag; a moderator answers within a week where possible, best-effort.

Discord and other places

If the operator runs a Discord server or another community space, these guidelines apply there as well, together with that platform's own rules.
